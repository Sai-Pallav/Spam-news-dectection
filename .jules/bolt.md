## 2024-05-15 - Memory optimizations for TF-IDF vectors
**Learning:** Dense representations (.toarray()) of TfidfVectorizer results consume massive amounts of memory, especially with `max_features=50000`, causing Out of Memory (OOM) issues or severe slowdowns when trying to convert them to pandas DataFrames. Additionally, using `fit_transform` on the test data causes data leakage and breaks the test metrics. Scikit-learn algorithms (LogisticRegression, Support Vector Regression, MultinomialNB) work perfectly out of the box with the sparse matrices returned by TfidfVectorizer.
**Action:** Do not use `.toarray()` or convert sparse matrices to dense representations like `pd.DataFrame` unless absolutely necessary. Maintain sparse matrix formats, and use `.transform()` on test sets instead of `.fit_transform()`.
